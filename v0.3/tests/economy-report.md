## Method

**What is real code.** Accounts start fresh (`IT.newGame()`: 250g, 3 permits, 0 heroes) and are played
by a policy bot through the actual module APIs. Combat runs the full v0.7 kit engine (traits,
reactions, statuses, floor rules, Hollow King adds/drain, Executioner mark/execute) with the two
official test hooks: `IT.combat.FAST` (no delays) and `IT.combat.auto`/`autoCmd` (Master choices).
Event nodes run `IT.events.pickEvent` → option → `IT.decide` → `IT.events.resolve` — the same
decision + resolution functions the DOM flow calls (the events *renderer* is DOM-bound, the logic is not).

**What is replicated from `js/ui.js`** (the flow layer — ui.js cannot run headless): the exact
application order of `startExpedition` → `enterNode` → `nodeDone`/`applyEffects` → `_afterCombat` →
`completeNode` → `finishExpedition`, including win-bonus exp (floor×15), survivor shock on death
(fear+15 / loyalty−8), DARKNESS entry fear+5, node-enemy threat scaling (0.925–1.225×), treasure
30–70g, rest-node heal 35%/fear−10, knowledge unlocks after F10/F20 boss fights, and clear rewards
via `IT.floorClearGold` / `IT.floorClearPermits` / master exp `n×8 (+60 at F10/F20)`.

**Documented approximations** (driver-side, all listed):
1. Event-node loot is NOT approximated — it is the real `IT.events.resolve` output (gold ranges
   5–90 by event: chest/robbery 40–90, doors-left 15–30, shrine-loot 20–45, corpse-bury 5–15, fork 5–20).
   Only the *option choice* is a policy heuristic (safe/greedy/mixed per policy, cost-gated like the UI).
2. Remains nodes: gear of the fallen is auto-recovered and equipped into empty slots (stat effect real;
   bury-rites loyalty path not taken). Master Commands are policy-invariant (OVERDRIVE r2+ vs boss/3+;
   PROTECT Tank below 40% party HP; SACRIFICE never).
3. In-combat Retreat is never chosen (policies express caution via scouting/resting instead); the
   round-60 expulsion and per-hero withdraw reactions still fire on their own.
4. Party kept at best-3 alive (1 Healer + 1 Tank preferred); floor order sequential, lowest uncleared
   first (master-level gates never bound in practice: ML3 lands ~F9).

**Policies.** CAREFUL: scouts every reachable node while gold lasts, rests at party avg <70% (or any
hero <35%), safe event options, Defend stances in combat. GREEDY: never scouts, rests only when
someone is ≤25% HP, always takes the money option, Focus/Push-on in combat. BALANCED: scouts 50%,
rests at avg <60% (any <30%), mixed event choices. Shared: keep ≥3 living heroes (permits first,
then gold), death → replace and continue. Account ends at first F20 clear or 60 expeditions.

**Tuning changes now LIVE in js/ and mirrored 1:1 by this driver** (this sweep is the AFTER state):
1. Scout cost 10g flat, 25g on DARKNESS F11-13 (was 25/50) — driver reads it live via IT.map.scoutCost.
2. Survivor's purse: a Floor-10 expedition ending with no win AND no hero death pays +175g +1 permit
   (mirrored exactly from ui.js finishExpedition, counted as income category "purse" on F10).
3. Hero-side burn now decays (FYI — nothing applies burn to heroes in v0.7, so no driver impact; it
   runs inside the real combat engine regardless).

## CHOKE POINTS

1. **F10 "The Wall" is THE choke — it eats the whole mid-game.** 66.8% of all hero deaths in the sweep happen on F10 (134471 of 201450). Per-attempt clear rate there is 9.9% and per-attempt wipe rate 66.9% — accounts grind it ~10.1 attempts per successful clear. Within the 60-expedition budget only 46.6% (CAREFUL) / 56.5% (BALANCED) / 63.3% (GREEDY) of accounts ever break the Wall.

2. **F10 runs a hard negative gold balance and kills accounts by bankruptcy.** Net per F10 expedition: -70.8g (CAREFUL), -67.3g (BALANCED), -64.0g (GREEDY). Failed F10 attempts pay ~zero (clear bounty is win-only) while each death pushes a 120g/1-permit replacement: recruit spend on F10 reaches 190.5g/expedition for GREEDY. The CAREFUL death spiral ends in bankruptcy for 60.1% of accounts (mean final gold 2428g vs GREEDY 3985g).

3. **Scout economics after the 10g/25g price cut.** CAREFUL now pays 58.1g/expedition on scouts (23.8% of gross income, was 126.8g at the 25/50 price). F1 net is now 40.0g/exp and DARKNESS F11 nets 16.9g/exp for CAREFUL vs 63.9g/exp for unspecting GREEDY — the flat floors no longer punish scouting, but the F11-13 premium (25g) still leaves the informed player behind the blind one there.

4. **F9-F13 is the secondary danger band; F14+ snowballs.** Wipe rates climb from ~0 at F5 to 21.4% at F9 and 44.5% at F11, then fall by half or more for the survivors (10.9% at F15). Deep-floor net gold turns strongly positive — F14-F20 run 348.2g to 655.7g net/expedition (BALANCED) — the clear bounty (40+25n) outpaces every cost once the party is past the filter. The economy does not choke deep; **survival at F9-F13 is the only real gate, and F10 is the crusher.**

5. **Rest is a dead lever; permits were the Wall's second currency and the purse fixed them.** Lobby rest fires only 0.2/10 expeditions (2.1g/exp): Wall damage is lethal (mark→execute kills from any HP), not attritional, so there is nothing to rest back. Permits: CAREFUL now earns 33713 vs 25115 spent on recruits (BEFORE the purse: 20285 in / 23825 out — a deficit that converted directly into 120g gold recruits); the attempt-paid purse flipped every policy to a surplus.

## Tuning recommendations (numbers only — nothing tuned here)

*Status: recommendation 1a (survivor's purse, 175g+1 permit) and 2a (10g flat scout, 25g on F11-13)*
*are now LIVE in js/ — the BEFORE/AFTER section measures their effect. Still open: 1b, 2b, 3, 4, 5.*

1. **Soften the F10 attempt tax.** Today the Wall costs ~10.1 attempts per clear and 66.9% wipes/attempt with zero income on failure. Options with numbers: (a) pay a "survivor's purse" of 150-200g for a no-death loss at F10 (roughly half the recruit treadmill), or (b) raise the informed per-attempt clear rate from the current ~9.9% to 20-25% (≈4-5 attempts per clear) by shaving Executioner HP 1300→1100 or stretching the mark→execute cycle 3→4 turns for parties that already know the fight.

2. **Make scouting buy something.** At 25g (50g on F11-13) the information is worth less than the reroute saves. Either (a) cut cost to 10g flat (CAREFUL spend drops from ~58.1g/exp to ~40g/exp), or (b) keep 25g and let a scouted combat node lose ~1 threat star (prepared approach), converting gold into measurable damage reduction. Also gate the "scout everything" affordance early: scouting before F3 is strictly negative-EV (40.0g/exp at F1).

3. **Pay permits on attempts, not only clears, from F9 up.** A failed F10/11/12 attempt currently returns 0 permits while burning 1-3 replacements. CAREFUL runs a deficit of -8598 permits against 21019 F10 attempts — so a "veteran pay" of **+1 permit per boss attempt that ends with no deaths** (or flatly +1 every 4th boss attempt, ~0.25/attempt) closes the gap without touching clear rewards or inflating GREEDY's surplus.

4. **Add a death-streak brake on boss floors.** 66.8% of all deaths sit on one floor; the accounts that fail F10 five-plus times are the ones going bankrupt (60.1% of CAREFUL). A stacking "the Tower grows bored" rule — each consecutive F10 wipe applies −8% boss HP cumulatively up to −40% on the 5th attempt, resetting on clear — converts hopeless grinds into slow wins without touching first-try difficulty.

5. **Re-balance deep-floor windfalls or embrace them.** F14-F20 run +348.2g to +655.7g net/expedition for survivors. If deep floors should still press, either flatten clear gold to 40+18n past F13 (F20: 900→400) or add a deep-supply drain (e.g. torches at 30g/expedition on F11+). If the intended funnel is "F10 is the only wall," leave it — but note the ML4/ML5 cost discounts (rest ×0.6, recruit 90g) arrive too late to matter: the median account ends at ML3.
