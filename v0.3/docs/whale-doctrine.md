# The Whale Doctrine — Infinite Tower

Design rule, locked before monetization is ever designed. Every future
monetization idea is checked against this document; anything that violates it
is rejected no matter how much revenue it projects.

## The principle

> **Money can buy Heroes. Money cannot buy Knowledge. Money cannot buy Bonds.**

| Layer | Bought by | Ceiling for a whale |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadth** — roster size, options, classes | Money (gacha) | Full roster. This is fine and intended. |
| **Knowledge** — boss mechanics, floor rules, counters, the Executioner's mark | Gameplay only (deaths, attempts, Tower Analysis) | None. A whale walks into Floor 10 blind and dies like everyone else. |
| **Bonds** — relationships, memories, grief, gear of the fallen | Time + interaction only | None. Mira II is not Mira, at any price. |

## Why this works commercially (not just morally)

The game's tested emotional core is **loss + learning**. Both are non-purchasable
by construction:

1. **Permadeath is sacred.** No revive purchases, no timers, no buy-back.
   Buying a hero back would delete the only mechanic that makes a 1★ valuable.
2. **Knowledge progression is account-wide but earned in blood.** The Wall
   teaches via casualties; the Hollow King demands loyalty raised through
   campfires and burial rites. Neither accepts payment.
3. **Bonds and memories cannot be granted.** They are built by expeditions
   survived together. `Carried by Sera (†F1)` has no cash price.

A whale therefore buys **more chances to feel the thing the game sells** —
a deeper bench for the next funeral — not a shortcut past it.

## Hard rules for future monetization design

1. **Sell breadth, never power past the knowledge/bond curve.** Paid heroes may
   start stronger (rarity already does) but must face the same walls, rules,
   and drains as anyone else.
2. **Never sell:** revives, floor skips, knowledge unlocks, bond/memory
   items, retreat insurance, execute protection.
3. **No pain-removal products.** Grief and loss are the product. Anything that
   mutes them competes with our own game.
4. **Dup/dupe value must be roster-side** (permits, cosmetics, master xp at
   most) — never knowledge-side.
5. **The whale end-state must still be the game.** A player who owns everything
   still walks the Deep Tower with three people they chose, and the Tower can
   still take all three. If that sentence ever stops being true, the game is
   dead regardless of revenue.

## The test

Any monetization proposal must answer:

> "After this purchase, does the player still meet the Wall blind,
> and does the funeral still hurt?"

If yes → allowed. If no → rejected.
